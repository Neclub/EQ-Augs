I would make **one enhancement** to this design: **split the weights into two layers**.

* **Layer 1:** Role weights (Tank, Priest, Caster, Melee, Hybrid)
* **Layer 2:** Class modifiers (Warrior, SK, Paladin, etc.)

For example:

```text
Tank Base
----------
AC             10
HP              9
Heroic DEX      8
Heroic STA      7

Warrior Modifier
----------------
AC            +1
Heroic DEX    +1

Shadow Knight Modifier
----------------------
Mana          +2
Spell Damage  +1

Paladin Modifier
----------------
Heal Amount   +2
Mana          +2
```

This approach means if the EverQuest meta changes (for example, Heroic Agility becomes more valuable for tanks in a future expansion), you only update the **Tank Base** profile rather than editing three separate class profiles. It's significantly easier to maintain over multiple expansions.
